# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language used for buying courses and getting money back.

## Buying

**Purchase**:
A single payment one user makes for one course, recording the amount paid.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course. An approved Refund ends it.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase of several Seats in one course, bought for a team rather than for the buyer.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence. A team member claims it with a coupon code.

**Revenue**:
The amount paid on Purchases, less approved Refunds.

## Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a written reason, for the money paid on a Purchase back. It is Requested until an Admin settles it, either Approved or Declined. A Decline is final for that Purchase, and the learner cannot withdraw a request.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back on a Purchase when an Admin approves its Refund Request. It is always the full amount paid on that Purchase, never part of it and never the course's list price. A learner gets at most one Refund per course. Cadence records the Refund, but the money moves outside Cadence. It ends the learner's Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Reversal, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days (UTC) after a Purchase, inclusive of the fourteenth, during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request raised, judged only at the moment of raising it. The Purchase must be inside its Refund Window, with under two hours watched, under half the lessons completed, not a Team Licence, no Refund Request already raised on it, and no earlier Refund for the same course.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the payer raises with their card issuer, outside Cadence. Cadence does not model Chargebacks, and the word never means a Refund Request.
